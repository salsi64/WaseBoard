Get started

Ready in three steps

  1. Install or open

    Download the Windows app, or simply open the web version: it is the same sound catalog.

    ⬇ Windows🌐 Web

  2. Sign in with Discord

    Your account is enough: it is used to show your avatar and find your voice channel. Nothing else to type.

    ➕ The bot is not on my server

  3. Join voice, click

    Join a voice channel, click “🔊 Join my voice channel” (or type /join), then click a sound: everyone hears it.

Features

Everything for a voice channel that has fun

🎞

Last-seconds clip

A shortcut keeps what just played on your PC (game, video, Discord) and turns it into a sound.

✏️

Sound editor

Name, emoji, color, volume from 0 to 200%, trimming on the waveform, file replacement.

⌨️

Discord commands

/play with search, /stop, /top and /bilan: without opening the app.

🎮

In-game mic

Your sounds also go through your microphone: your teammates hear them in Valorant, CS2, Fortnite…

🎞 Something funny just happened? Keep it with one shortcut

WaseBoard holds the last 10, 15 or 30 seconds of your PC's sound in memory. Press a shortcut and the add window opens: trim it, name it, it's a sound. Off by default; nothing is written to disk until you ask, and a red light shows when the capture is running.

Do you run a Discord server?

How it works on the server side

The Discord bot and the app connect to the same place: a “WaseBoard server”, which hosts the sound catalog. The one behind this page is public and free.

RECOMMENDED

⚡ Use the public instance

Add the bot to your Discord, download the client, sign in: that's all.

  • Free, online right now
  • No technical skills required
  • A separate sound library for each server
